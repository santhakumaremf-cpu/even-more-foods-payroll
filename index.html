Key Formulas (from Excel)

Working Days = 30 − 4 Sundays − 0 NH = 26
PF: IF EPF=Y → IF Basic>15000 THEN ₹1800 ELSE ROUNDUP(Basic×12%)
ESI: IF ESI=Y AND (Basic/(Paid−Sundays))>176 AND Basic≤21000 → ROUNDUP(Basic×0.75%)
OT = ROUND((Gross/30/8)×Hours, 0)
Night Incentive = Shifts × ₹40
Net = Total − PF − ESI − Food − Advance − Reimbursement
High wage split: Basic 50% · HRA 20% · SPL 30%. Other workers: full amount as Basic after LOP.
